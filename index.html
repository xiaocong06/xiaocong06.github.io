I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Statistics at the University of Pittsburgh.

My research interests broadly lie in random matrix theory, as well as the mathematical foundations and algorithmic aspects of high-dimensional statistics. I am also interested in the design and analysis of sequential decision-making methods under uncertainty, particularly in revenue management applications such as inventory control and dynamic pricing.
