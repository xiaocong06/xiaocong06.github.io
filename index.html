Welcome to my homepage! I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Marshall School of Business at the University of Southern California, mentored by Prof. Yingying Fan and Prof. Jinchi Lv. I received my Ph.D. degree in 2024 from the Department of Mathematics at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where I was advised by Prof. Zhigang Bao. I was also fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Qiyang Han on specific projects. My research interests primarily revolve around random matrix theory and high-dimensional statistics.

I am currently on the 2025–2026 academic job market and would be happy to discuss any opportunities!
